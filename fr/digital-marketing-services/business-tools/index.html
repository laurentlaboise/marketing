AI business tools

CRM, email automation, lead management, reporting, and AI Strategist.

Marketing work stalls when leads live in chat, email sits in a personal inbox, and no one sees last month’s numbers. We set up the tools that sit in the WordsThatSells portal: CRM, email automation, lead management, reporting, and the AI Strategist.

What you get

  • CRM to hold contacts, deals, and follow-ups
  • Email automation for newsletters and nurture sequences
  • Lead management from forms, Facebook, and the website
  • Reporting your team can read without a specialist
  • Portal AI Strategist for briefs, priorities, and next steps

How delivery works

Automatisation Pro ($1,099/mo) is the plan built around these tools. Growth Engine ($649) and Digital Footprint ($329) can add selected automation. Sign in to the portal to connect accounts and review reports. Affiliates and agencies can offer the same stack white-label.
